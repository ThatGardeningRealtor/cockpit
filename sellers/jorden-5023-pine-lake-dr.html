Your Options

List on the MarketRecommended

Price it right, attract qualified buyers, maximize what you walk away with. With solid equity and a realistic price point, this is the clearest path to closing clean and moving forward. Timeline: 30–60 days to contract.

Wait and WatchCaution

The market is stable now but not guaranteed to improve. Holding longer means continued carrying costs — mortgage, taxes, insurance, maintenance. Only makes sense if there's a specific reason to wait.

Sell Off-Market / InvestorQuick Exit

Fastest path — typically 7–21 days to close, no showings, no repairs. Trade-off is a lower offer, usually 10–15% below market. Best for someone who values speed and certainty over top dollar.
